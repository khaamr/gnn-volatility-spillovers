Each circle is a stock. Its signal represents a scaled, past volatility feature. On their own, these observations describe each stock separately.

Our study uses 27 stocks. This miniature example uses six so you can follow every connection.

02 / connect

learn which stocks
belong together.

A connection tells the model where to share information. The study compares sector, complete, empty and graphical-lasso stock graphs.

Graphical lasso estimates a sparse network of conditional relationships from past data. An edge is a statistical connection; it does not establish that one company causes another to move.

03 / aggregate

let the neighbors
add context.

Small moving signals show one aggregation pass into the selected stock. Each neighbor contributes to a graph feature, alongside the stock’s own historical features.

Select a node in the diagram. Change its signal below, then select a neighbor to see how that neighbor’s aggregate changes.

neighbor context for A
(B + C + D) / 3 = 0.283

This demo uses equal neighbor weights: (Wx)ᵢ = Σⱼ Wᵢⱼ xⱼ. A moving dot represents information aggregation, not a causal shock or a new time observation.

04 / lift

a stock pair
becomes a node.

Covariances also depend on how pairs move together. In a line graph, each node represents a stock pair. Two pair nodes connect whenever they share a stock.

Watch A–B: it connects to A–C and A–D through A, and to B–C and B–E through B.

A–B ↔ A–C: shared stock A
A–B ↔ B–E: shared stock B
A–B ⤬ E–F: no shared stock
stock-pair node

For readability, this shows the line graph of the eight illustrated edges. The study uses all 351 unordered pairs of its 27 stocks.

05 / assemble

from relationships
to a risk matrix.

The model forecasts variances on the stock graph and correlations on the pair graph, then reconstructs a full covariance matrix. Diagonal cells describe individual variances; off-diagonal cells describe co-movement.

Σ̂ = D̂ R̂ D̂
variances + correlations → covariance

That matrix feeds portfolio optimization. Lower forecast loss and lower realized portfolio risk are separate outcomes to test.

The displayed matrix is a positive-definite toy construction from the illustrated graph and input values. It is not produced by the fitted HAR models or the spectral extension.
